LIMITS OF THIS FIRST-STAGE PILOT
==============================

This project tests compact feature encoders on cached particle episodes. It does
not integrate them into full drifting/Riesz generator training and does not
establish ImageNet generation quality, FID parity, or an end-to-end speedup.

A synthetic cache verifies software wiring and numerical behavior only. It is
not evidence about semantic image features or scientific success. Real runs
require the user's compatible local MAE checkpoint/source checkout and real
generated particle snapshots. No pretrained teacher is bundled or downloaded.

Teacher features are computed up front and stored. That cache has construction,
storage and data-read costs, and covers only its recorded particles, snapshots,
CFG settings, classes and noise identities. Removing online teacher calls in
this pilot does not make teacher pretraining/cache construction free. Generality
to future generator distributions is unproven. Held-out real/noise IDs and a
held-out generator checkpoint are required to probe that limitation.

Feature matching and kernel-geometry matching retain the same adapter heads and
teacher-channel output widths. The reconstruction control uses its native encoder
channels. Channel widths may therefore differ for diagnostics, which compare
normalized within-encoder geometry rather than direct coordinate differences.
For fair cost comparisons, count retained adapters and the actual feature branch
operations used at deployment, together with backward-to-input cost.

The real cache adapter exposes five common teacher maps: conv1 and layer1-4.
This is not the original full MAE loss feature collection: intermediate residual
block activations and local-patch statistics are not all reproduced. A positive
result here must be followed by a matched comparison against the complete
original training recipe.

Nearest-neighbor overlap and normalized pairwise geometry can look good without
preserving the encoder Jacobian. They do not prove useful pixel/latent gradients,
conservative drift, faithful generator updates, or semantic quality. Numerical
collapse checks identify degeneracy, not whether a representation captures the
right image distinctions. Stable index tie-breaking can inflate overlap when
features collapse; always inspect spread/collapse diagnostics alongside overlap.

Effective-neighbor counts here use exp(Shannon entropy) of normalized-distance
Laplace-style affinities. They describe selectivity at each temperature, not the
entire normalized Riesz field or its backward pass. The within-group variance
fraction depends on the feature origin and is a shortcut diagnostic, not a
translation-invariant quality metric. All model selection must use validation
episodes; audit episodes are held out from tuning.
